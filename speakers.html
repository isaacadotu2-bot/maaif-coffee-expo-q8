Call for Papers

We invite researchers, farmers, and industry professionals to submit abstracts on coffee production, processing, marketing, and digital innovation. Abstracts should be no more than 300 words and must include the author's name, organisation, and contact email.

Accepted papers will be presented during the Research Showcase on 18 September 2026. Submissions are reviewed by a panel of experts, and authors will be notified of the outcome by email.

Milestone Date Status
Abstract submission deadline 31 May 2026 Closed

Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo

About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo brings together farmers, exporters, roasters, researchers, and policymakers to share ideas and build partnerships across the coffee value chain. Organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF), the expo is a fictional event created for this examination.

Over three days, visitors can explore exhibition booths, attend expert talks, join cupping sessions, and meet buyers from around the world. The expo highlights Uganda's growing role in specialty coffee and the opportunities available to young entrepreneurs and smallholder farmers.
